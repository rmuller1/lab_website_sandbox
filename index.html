We study how RNA-binding proteins decide which transcripts a cell translates, and how those decisions commit a progenitor to one fate over another.

Research

Three questions we keep coming back to

Ribosome profiling · CLIP

Selective translation in progenitors

Transcript abundance predicts protein output poorly in dividing progenitors. We map where that decoupling happens and which RBPs impose it.

Live imaging · zebrafish

Timing of fate commitment

Following single cells from the last symmetric division onward, to ask whether commitment is a threshold event or a slow drift.

Organoids · optogenetics

Perturbing condensates on demand

Light-inducible clustering lets us assemble and dissolve RNP granules on a scale of seconds and read out the translational consequences.
